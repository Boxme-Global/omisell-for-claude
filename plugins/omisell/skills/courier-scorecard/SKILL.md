# Omisell: courier scorecard

Goal: the user can see which carrier delivers most reliably and fastest, to decide where to route parcels.

## Steps

Window: the user's period, else the last 30 days (a carrier comparison over 7 days is too thin; say which you used). Max 90 days.

1. **Outcomes.** `courier_performance` with `date_from` / `date_to` (unix seconds) or `period` (`last_30_days`, `this_month`, `last_month`...), `limit` 20. Rows: `courier` (the real carrier), `total`, `delivered`, `cancelled`, `success_rate` (0-1, delivered / total). Counts shipments created in the window.
2. **Speed and returns.** `fulfillment_lead_time` with `date_from`, `date_to` (both required, unix seconds) and `group_by: "courier"`. Per carrier (`courier_name`): `count_order`, `count_order_return` (failed delivery, sent back), and average seconds `avg_shipped_order` (to hand-over), `avg_delivery_order` (hand-over to delivered: the carrier's own time), `avg_success_order` (end to end). Counts orders created in the window; ignore its money fields here.
3. Optional, only if the user asks about one channel or store: repeat step 2 with `store_ids`.

Join the two results on the carrier name. A carrier missing from one side gets "-" there, never 0. A row named "N/A" is orders with no carrier yet; leave it out of the ranking and mention it. Likewise a `courier_performance` row named after a booking gateway (e.g. Boxme) with no matching lead-time row is parcels whose real carrier is not known yet: leave it out of the ranking, mention its count.

## Answer

- Summary: best and worst carrier on success rate, and the fastest on hand-over-to-delivered, with the window.
- Table (top 8 by parcels): carrier · parcels · delivered % · cancelled % · return % (`count_order_return / count_order`) · avg hand-over → delivered (hours, or days above 48 h) · avg end to end. Show percentages with one decimal.
- Note under the table: the first columns count shipments, the time and return columns count orders; small carriers (under 30 parcels) are not reliable to rank.
- Next: route more volume to the best carrier in the Omisell shipping rules; check stuck parcels of the weakest with `/omisell:stuck-shipments`; look at one late parcel with `/omisell:track-shipment`.

## Do not

- Do not rank carriers on fewer than 30 parcels without saying so.
- Do not present the booking gateway (e.g. a Boxme booking) as the carrier; use the names the tools return.
- Do not mix in revenue or shipping-fee figures; this skill is about delivery service.
