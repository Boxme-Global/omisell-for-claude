# Omisell: gross margin

Goal: the owner or accountant sees which SKUs earn and which lose money in the period, and which have no cost recorded.

## Steps

1. **Period.** Unix seconds in the business time zone, **at most 31 days**, filtered on the **order creation date**. For a longer period, answer the latest 31 days and offer the rest.
2. **Profit.** Call `gross_profit_by_sku` with `date_from`, `date_to`, `limit` 20 (max 50) and `sort`:
   - `"desc"` (default): most profitable first,
   - `"asc"`: loss-makers / thinnest margin first - use when the user asks "which SKUs lose money".
   Add `skus` to look at named SKUs only. It returns `rows` (SKU, name, currency, net quantity, net sales, cogs, gross profit, margin %), `truncated`, `totals` (one per currency), `cogs_missing` and a `note`.
3. **By channel.** The tool has no channel grouping. When the user asks per channel, call `list_stores`, then `gross_profit_by_sku` once per store with `store_id`, `limit` 5 - at most 4 stores in one turn; offer the rest.
4. **Refused.** `missing_permission` → "This needs the profit report permission (or Cost management) in Omisell." There is no fallback.

## Definitions (from the tool)

- Gross profit = net sales (returned lines excluded) − cost of goods recorded at the time of sale; margin % = gross profit / net sales. Same formula as the Omisell finance overview.
- Cost of goods comes from Omisell Cost management. A SKU with sales and cost 0 is counted in `cogs_missing`: its profit is overstated.
- Totals are per currency; never add two currencies together.

## Answer

- Summary: total net sales, gross profit and margin %, with the period ("orders created 06/10-05/11/2026"); and the count of SKUs missing a cost if `cogs_missing` > 0.
- Table: top 10 SKUs - SKU, name, quantity, net sales, cost, gross profit, margin %. The per-SKU margin % column is required on every row (from that row's `margin`); a total margin alone is not enough. A cost-0 row shows "—" for margin. With `sort` asc, title it "lowest profit first". Mark cost-0 rows as "no cost recorded". If `truncated`, say more SKUs exist.
- Next: enter missing costs in Omisell (Finance > Cost management); fees eating the margin `/omisell:fees-by-channel`; revenue for the same period `/omisell:revenue-report`.

## Do not

- Do not subtract marketplace fees, shipping or ads to make a "net profit": the tool does not, and a mixed figure misleads.
- Do not call a SKU loss-making when its cost is missing; call it "cost not recorded".
