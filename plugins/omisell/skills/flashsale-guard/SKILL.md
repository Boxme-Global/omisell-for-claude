# Omisell: flash sale guard

Goal: catch a wrong flash sale price or a SKU that will sell out before the sale goes live, while it can still be fixed.

## Steps

Budget: 6 calls: 1 + 1 + up to 2 sales in step 3 + up to 2 SKUs in step 4. Never ask the user for SKUs: the sale itself lists them.

1. `find_flashsale_price_warnings` (pass `store_id` only if the user named a channel; `limit` 50, max 200). It covers running and upcoming flash sales only. An item is flagged when its flash price is at or below `threshold` × its normal price (`warning_rule`, default 50%), the same warning the Flash sale screen shows. Its `sku` is the CHANNEL listing SKU.
2. `list_promotions` with `kind: "flashsale"`, `state: "upcoming"`, `limit: 50` for start times and store names; match a row by `id` == an item's `flashsale_id`, never by name. A flagged item's `flashsale_status` gives its sale's state: 100 = upcoming, 200 = running (say it in words). If the user asks about a running sale, also call it with `state: "running"` in place of one step-3 call.
3. `flashsale_items` with `flashsale_id` for the sale that starts soonest (and the one the user named, if different): at most 2 sales. Per listing it returns `reserved` (registered for the sale), `sold`, `remaining`, `allocations` (warehouse SKU, warehouse name, `quantity` held) and `unallocated` (registered but not held for lack of stock). `unallocated` > 0 is a stock gap. `unallocated` = 0 does NOT prove the sale is stocked: a sale created on the marketplace always holds its full quantity (read the result's `note`). When `unallocated_hidden` is true the account is warehouse-limited: say the gap figure needs the shop owner, never treat null as 0.
4. `stock_on_hand` with `sku` = a warehouse SKU from `allocations[].sku`, for at most 2 SKUs, most urgent first: a positive `unallocated`, then the largest `remaining`, then the deepest discount from step 1. Read `available_now` (can ship now, incoming excluded), not `quantity`. Offer to check the rest.
5. A refusal on `list_promotions` / `flashsale_items` (Flash sale permission) does not stop the price check: report step 1 and say the stock check needs the Flash sale permission.

## Answer

- Summary: how many flash sale items look mispriced (`total`; say if `truncated`) and how many checked SKUs have no or low stock, with the soonest affected sale and its start time when known.
- Price table: SKU · Flash sale · Starts (when known) · Normal price · Flash price · Discount % (`per_discount`), deepest discount first, top 10. Add the store name only when step 2 or the user already gave it; never show a store id.
- Stock table: listing SKU · Registered · Sold · Remaining · Not held (`unallocated`) · Warehouse SKU · Available now. Mark a row red when `unallocated` > 0 or `available_now` is 0 in every warehouse.
- Next: fix prices or quantities in Omisell's Flash sale screen before the start time; reorder → `/omisell:reorder-plan`; after the sale → `/omisell:campaign-performance`.

## Do not

- Do not change a flash sale price or switch a sale off; this round has no promotion write tool.
- Do not call a deep discount a mistake as fact: it crossed the warning threshold, the seller decides.
- Do not call `stock_on_hand` with a channel SKU, and do not loop it over every SKU.
- Do not ask the user which SKUs to check: take them from `flashsale_items`.
- Do not say no flash sale is running unless you listed `state: "running"`: step 2 lists upcoming sales only, so say nothing about running sales you did not check.
